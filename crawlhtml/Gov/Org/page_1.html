Understanding Cherry Switches: A Brief Overview #understanding-cherry-switches-a-brief-overview










Cherry MX switches, a staple in the mechanical keyboard world, are renowned for their durability, distinct feel, and unfortunately, sometimes their noise.

But why are some Cherry MX switches louder than others? 🤔

The answer lies in their mechanical design. Each keystroke involves the stem moving within the switch housing, potentially colliding with the top and bottom housings, creating that distinctive “click” or “thud.”

Cherry offers a variety of switch types categorized by their color, each offering a unique typing experience and sound profile.

The Science Behind Switch Noise: What Makes Them Loud or Quiet? #the-science-behind-switch-noise-what-makes-them-loud-or-quiet










The sound of a mechanical keyboard switch is generated by a combination of factors:

  • Bottom Out: The sound produced when the keycap reaches the bottom of its travel.
  • Top Out: The sound produced when the keycap is released and returns to its resting position.
  • Spring Noise: The sound of the spring expanding and contracting during keystrokes.
  • Stabilizer Rattle: Noise generated by the stabilizers used on larger keys like the spacebar and enter key.

Factors Affecting Switch Noise:

  • Switch Type: Linear switches are generally quieter than tactile or clicky switches.
  • Actuation Force: Switches with higher actuation forces tend to be louder.
  • Dampening: Switches with integrated dampeners, like Cherry MX Silent, are designed to reduce noise.
  • Materials: The materials used in the switch housing, stem, and spring can impact the sound.
  • Lubrication: Applying lubricant to the switch components can reduce friction and noise.

Tips for Reducing Keyboard Noise #tips-for-reducing-keyboard-noise










Even with the quietest Cherry MX switches, you can take additional steps to minimize keyboard noise:

  • Choose a Solid Keyboard Case: A sturdy case made from materials like aluminum or wood can help dampen sound.
  • Use Thick PBT Keycaps: PBT plastic is known for its sound-dampening properties compared to ABS plastic.
  • Invest in a Desk Mat: A soft desk mat can absorb vibrations and reduce noise.
  • Lubricate Your Switches: Applying lubricant to the switch components can significantly reduce friction and noise.
  • Add Sound Dampening Material: Consider adding foam or rubber sheets inside your keyboard case to further absorb sound.
  • Adjust Your Typing Technique: Typing more gently and avoiding bottoming out the keys can make a noticeable difference.

Maintenance and Care for Cherry Switches #maintenance-and-care-for-cherry-switches










Cherry MX switches are known for their durability, but proper maintenance can extend their lifespan and keep them feeling their best:

  • Regular Cleaning: Dust, dirt, and debris can accumulate under the keycaps, affecting switch performance. Use compressed air or a soft brush to clean your keyboard regularly.
  • Avoid Spills: Liquids can damage mechanical switches. Be careful not to spill drinks or food on your keyboard.
  • Store Properly: If you’re not using your keyboard for an extended period, store it in a cool, dry place.

By following these tips, you can ensure that your Cherry MX switches continue to provide a satisfying and (hopefully quiet!) typing experience for years to come.

FAQ #faq

What makes Cherry MX Silent Red switches quieter than other mechanical keyboard switches? #what-makes-cherry-mx-silent-red-switches-quieter-than-other-mechanical-keyboard-switches

Cherry MX Silent Red switches are designed with integrated dampeners that absorb the sound produced during both the bottom-out and upstroke phases of a keystroke. This unique design minimizes the noise typically associated with mechanical switches, making them significantly quieter than traditional Cherry MX switches, which lack these dampeners.

How do Cherry MX Silent Black switches compare in noise level to Cherry MX Silent Red switches? #how-do-cherry-mx-silent-black-switches-compare-in-noise-level-to-cherry-mx-silent-red-switches

While both switches feature integrated dampeners, Cherry MX Silent Blacks are slightly louder than Silent Reds due to their heavier actuation force of 60g compared to Silent Red’s 45g. This means that while both are quiet, Silent Reds are the quieter option overall.

Are there any modifications I can make to Cherry switches to further reduce their noise? #are-there-any-modifications-i-can-make-to-cherry-switches-to-further-reduce-their-noise

Yes! You can apply lubricants to the switch components to reduce friction and noise. Additionally, using sound-dampening materials inside the keyboard case, such as foam or rubber, can help absorb sound. Some enthusiasts also modify switches with films or pads to further enhance their quietness.

Do Cherry MX Silent switches sacrifice typing feel for reduced noise? #do-cherry-mx-silent-switches-sacrifice-typing-feel-for-reduced-noise

Not necessarily! While some users may find the linear feel of Silent switches less tactile compared to tactile switches like Browns or Clears, many users appreciate the smoothness and responsiveness of Silent Reds and Blacks. Ultimately, it comes down to personal preference.

What are the quietest alternatives to Cherry MX Silent switches for mechanical keyboards? #what-are-the-quietest-alternatives-to-cherry-mx-silent-switches-for-mechanical-keyboards

Alternatives include Gateron Silent switches, which are known for their smooth and quiet operation, and Kailh BOX Silent switches, which offer a unique tactile feedback while maintaining a relatively quiet operation. NovelKeys Silk switches are also a great option for those seeking ultra-quiet performance.

How does the keyboard case material affect the overall noise level when using Cherry MX Silent switches? #how-does-the-keyboard-case-material-affect-the-overall-noise-level-when-using-cherry-mx-silent-switches

The material of the keyboard case can significantly impact noise levels. Metal cases tend to dampen sound better than plastic cases, while wooden cases can offer natural sound absorption. A solid, well-built case will help minimize vibrations and enhance the overall quietness of the keyboard.

Are Cherry MX Silent switches suitable for noise-sensitive environments like offices or libraries? #are-cherry-mx-silent-switches-suitable-for-noise-sensitive-environments-like-offices-or-libraries

Absolutely! Cherry MX Silent switches are specifically designed for quiet operation, making them ideal for noise-sensitive environments. Their integrated dampeners effectively reduce sound, allowing you to type without disturbing colleagues or fellow library-goers.

Where can I find sound tests comparing different Cherry MX Silent switches? #where-can-i-find-sound-tests-comparing-different-cherry-mx-silent-switches

You can find sound tests and comparisons on platforms like YouTube, where many keyboard enthusiasts conduct thorough reviews and sound tests of various switches. Additionally, websites like TipTapTypers provide valuable insights and comparisons of different Cherry MX switches.
